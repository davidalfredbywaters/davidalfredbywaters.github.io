### The daughter of an Irish attorney is taken up by a handsome but disgraced baronet.

***

*M. Hamilton was the pseudonym of Mary Churchill Luck, née Spottiswood-Ashe (1869-1949), who wrote some 20 novels between 1896 and 1928.  This, her first, was set, like many of her novels, in her native Ulster.*

“The characters are exceptionally distinct, the movement is brisk, and the dialogue is natural and convincing.” *Athenaeum*, February 15, 1896.

“A fine and mature performance”; the story is “told with great reticence, with insight and pathos, in a manner at once impersonal and sympathetic.” *Academy*, March 28, 1896

“A very pretty and natural book” with “a capitally thought-out character”; “The story abounds with fun, and is the pleasantest possible reading.” *Saturday Review*, April 4, 1896
