*It has lately come to my attention that the* New York Times*, despite its prominence as a publisher of crosswords, has published troublingly few crosswords by* me—*that is, crosswords* *with themes devised, grids constructed and filled, and clues written by* me*, in return for which sums of money are paid to* me*.*

*I don’t know why this is; I strongly suspect that the editor (who will go unnamed here) has something to do with it.  Anyway, it’s time to take action!  I hereby swear that, until the* New York Times *publishes another crossword by* me*, I will not publish another crossword in the* New York Times*!*
