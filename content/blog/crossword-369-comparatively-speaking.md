*Happiness is mainly a consequence of one’s standard of comparison.  If, say, you live in a three-bedroom house, you should compare it not with the six-bedroom house with indoor pool and backyard tennis court occupied by some undeserving  acquaintance, but with the basement studio apartment you occupied years ago; or, if you live in a basement studio apartment, with the rat-infested dungeon occupied by the falsely accused hero of the novel you’re reading; or, if you live in a rat-infested dungeon, with the circles of hell eternally occupied by the damned.*

*So it is with the crosswords on this site.  To enjoy them properly, you should compare the experience of solving them not with, say, a sunset cruise, or a gourmet meal, or a scenic walk in the company of one's ideal romantic partner, but with a fall down the stairs, or a kick in the teeth.  The clues all have answers and the answers all have clues, both answers and clues are largely free of typographical errors, and the squares are symmetrically arranged:  isn't this paradise enough?*
