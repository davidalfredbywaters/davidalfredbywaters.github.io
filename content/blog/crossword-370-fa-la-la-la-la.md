*Here’s something that’s bothered me for  years:  the notes in the Christmas carol “Deck the Halls” that are set to the words “fa la la la la la la la la” are in fact sung twice to the notes “re mi fa re mi re do ti do,” then to the notes “mi fa sol la ti do ti la sol,” and finally to the notes “la la la la sol fa mi re do.”  Of these thirty-six notes, then, only three (the ”la la la” that follow “fa” in the final chorus) correspond to the words to which they are sung.  Should we stand for this, ye lads and lasses?*
