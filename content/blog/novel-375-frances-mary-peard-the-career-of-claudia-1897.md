### An educated young woman attempts a career as a landscape gardener.

***

*Frances Mary Peard (1835-1923) wrote some forty novels, for adults and young adults.  She is not to be confused with last fortnight's novelist Rosa Caroline Praed, though both ended their lives in Torquay, where one likes to think of them exchanging mail delivered by accident.*

It shows "gentle irony tempered with compassion" in illustrating "the entire inadequacy of the higher education . . . to cope with the weapons wielded by men and women of the world"; it is a "charming and sympathetic novel.” *Spectator*, March 27, 1897.

"The story is told with animation and pervaded by a pleasant, wholesome spirit, which should make it very welcome to the girl readers for whom it is obviously written.”  *Athenaeum*, April 17, 1897
