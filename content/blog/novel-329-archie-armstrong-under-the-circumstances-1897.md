### A young woman finds herself disinherited in favor of her guardian’s housekeeper.

***

*Archie Armstrong (1859-1925), a journalist and editor, wrote one play, one story collection, and one novel, this one.*

Armstrong “can tell a story very pleasantly, and has an instinct for a plot.” *Academy*, March 6, 1897

A “well-told story of to-day, written in good English.” *Athenaeum*, March 20, 1897

“Distinctly good”; the heroine is “very lifelike.”  *Spectator*, October 9, 1897
