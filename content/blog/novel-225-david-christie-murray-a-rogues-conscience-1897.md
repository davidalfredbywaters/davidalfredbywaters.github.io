### A pair of British criminals try their fortunes in Canada.

***

*Here is another novel by Murray (see Novels* [*009*](/blog/2018/1/16/novel-009-david-christie-murray-hearts-1883)*,* [*068*](/blog/2019/3/5/david-christie-murray-josephs-coat-1881)*,* [*127*](/blog/2020/4/21/novel-127-david-christie-murray-val-strange-1882)*,* [*181*](/blog/2021/5/4/novel-181-david-christie-murray-first-person-singular-1886)*), filled with vivid characters and exciting adventures.*

“Slight as is the tale, its cleverly managed incidents, local colouring . . . and first-hand knowledge of some curiously freakish products of our complicated modern life will reward the rapid reader.” *Athenaeum,* May 15, 1897

“We found it excellent reading. . . A fine spirit of farcical humour makes all his rascality sympathetic.” *Saturday Review*, May 22, 1897

*A contrasting view:*

“As a whole, it does not seem to hang together very well.” *Spectator*, February 19, 1898
