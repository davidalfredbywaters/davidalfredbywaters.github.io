### Two virtuous young sisters fall in love with not-so-virtuous young men.

***

*Mary Eliza Bramston (1841-1912), under the name M. Bramston, published some fifty novels.  This one, with its unusually convincing portrayal of its characters' habitual mutual incomprehension, suggests that her obscurity was undeserved.*

“A well-told story.” *Reynold’s Newspaper*, June 9, 1872

It has “a great deal of quiet beauty”; “many a book of far more pretension has failed to interest us as” much as this. *Spectator*, December 14, 1872
