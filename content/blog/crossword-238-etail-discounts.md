*I’ve thought of converting this website to an “etail” enterprise—charging a dollar apiece for the crosswords, fifty cents per volume for the novel recommendations, and offering for sale an array of t-shirts, coffee mugs, and tote bags with my logo.  I’m pretty sure I’d get rich quick.  But I don’t do it.  Why?  Because I care about you, inadequately compensated solver.  It would be simply wrong to tempt you into squandering on crosswords what you might spend on winter fuel or shoes for the children.  I just can’t do it, and I won’t.*
